What's so special about Manipal?

Manipal is a vibrant, cosmopolitan university town perched on a plateau in coastal Karnataka, just a few kilometers from the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. Some key highlights:

  • ⭐️Global Student Hub: Home to the Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), it draws tens of thousands of students from over 60 countries, giving a small town an energetic, international feel.
  • ⭐️Ideal Location: Nestled right between the Malpe beach coastline and the lush hills of the Western Ghats, offering easy access to nature and sunsets at spots like End Point.
  • ⭐️Modern Infrastructure: Known for world-class medical facilities, clean green campuses, a thriving food scene, and a safe, walkable environment built around student life.

📍Hasta Shilpa Heritage Village Museum

This open-air museum preserves traditional South Indian architecture through restored historic houses, craft heritage displays, and intricate woodwork. It offers a cultural walk through regional history and design. Open Tuesday through Sunday with limited morning and afternoon entry slots. Ideal for photography, architectural study, and cultural exploration.

📍Arbi Falls

A seasonal waterfall tucked away in the greenery near Dasharath Nagar. It comes to life during and immediately following the monsoon season, serving as a scenic retreat. Surrounded by rocky terrain and natural forest paths. Best visited during or right after the monsoon for optimal water flow.
